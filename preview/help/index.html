Help & Safety

Everything you need to buy and sell safely on campus.

How UniMart works

Buying: browse or search → open a listing → message the seller → agree a price (or send an offer) → meet on campus → check the item → pay the seller directly.

Selling: verify your institute membership → add photos, a title, price and condition → preview → publish → answer messages → mark the item sold.

UniMart does not handle payments, delivery or refunds. Deals are between the two members.

Safe campus exchanges

  • Meet in a busy, public place on campus (library entrance, canteen, main gate) during the day.
  • Bring a friend for expensive items. Tell someone where you're going.
  • Inspect and test the item before paying. For electronics, check it powers on and matches the photos.
  • Pay only when you have the item in hand. Prefer UPI or cash at the meeting, never in advance.
  • Don't invite people to your hostel room or share your room number publicly.

Avoiding scams

  • Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords or bank details. UniMart staff will never ask for them.
  • Be wary of prices that are far too low, urgent pressure, or requests to move to another app immediately.
  • Never pay a “deposit” or “delivery fee” to reserve an item.
  • Don't scan QR codes to “receive” money; receiving money never needs your PIN.

Prohibited items

Don't list:

  • Alcohol, tobacco, vapes, drugs, medicines or anything requiring a prescription.
  • Weapons, explosives, fireworks or hazardous chemicals.
  • Stolen, counterfeit or pirated goods, including copied textbooks and software licences.
  • Exam papers, assignments or academic-misconduct services.
  • Animals, personal data, accounts, or anything illegal under Indian law or institute rules.
  • Lotteries, raffles or paid lucky draws.

Moderators may remove listings that break these rules.

Reporting abuse

Use Report listing on any product page, or Report this member in a chat. You can also block a member from the chat menu. Reports go to UniMart moderators. If you are in danger, contact campus security or call 112.

What the verified badge means

A Verified Member badge means UniMart confirmed the person belongs to that institute, usually through their institute email. It is not a guarantee of honesty or of an item's quality, and it doesn't mean the institute endorses UniMart.

Offers

On listings marked “offers accepted”, you can propose a lower price. The seller can accept, decline or counter. An accepted offer reserves the item while you arrange to meet; it is not a payment and not a binding contract. Offers expire after 48 hours. UniMart does not run auctions.

Privacy

Your email address and phone number are never shown on listings. Use UniMart Messages for first contact and only share other details when you're comfortable. See the privacy notice.

Platform limitations

UniMart is an independent student project, not a service of NIT Sikkim. It doesn't inspect items, guarantee transactions, handle payments or resolve disputes beyond removing content and suspending accounts that break the rules.
